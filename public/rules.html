Abuja Lifestyle house rules

Short on purpose.

Who can play

  • You must be 18 or older. The city has nightlife, dating energy and optional top-ups later.
  • One person, one player. Do not share accounts or sell them.

How to behave

  • Everyone you see is treated as a real person online, except people marked NPC.
  • No harassment, threats, hate, or unwanted sexual talk. Nothing involving children.
  • No scams, no begging for real money, no selling things outside the game.
  • Links and phone numbers are blocked in chat. Do not try to bypass that.
  • You can mute or report (when those buttons go live). Breaking rules can get you muted or removed.

Game money

  • Naira in the game is play money. It cannot be withdrawn or turned into real cash.
  • Any future top-ups will be optional and handled by a payment provider.

What we keep (this version)

  • Your player data lives in your browser (localStorage): name, look, money, stats, progress.
  • Clearing browser data removes guest progress. A proper account system can be added later.
  • We do not store passwords or payment details in this version.

Leaving

Use “New person” in More to start over. To fully remove data, clear site data for this domain in your browser.

These rules can change as the game grows. Continuing to play means you accept the current version.
